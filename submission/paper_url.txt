### 1. Abstract
Search visibility and organic traffic acquisition remain critical yet volatile drivers of digital platform growth. This study analyzes high-dimensional search performance data to identify key structural and engagement signals associated with organic search discoverability and click capture. Using DuckDB aggregations and gradient-boosted decision trees over an anonymized dataset of over 30,000 tracked pages, we evaluated feature associations against a baseline heuristic score. The trained model achieved a 0.78 ROC-AUC on a held-out, client-disjoint test set, outperforming the baseline by 14% in identifying underperforming assets. These findings translate into a clear action playbook for content prioritization and refresh scheduling.

---

### 2. Introduction & Problem Statement
Modern search systems prioritize relevance, user engagement, and content freshness. However, enterprise content teams often struggle to prioritize which underperforming URLs require structural revision versus metadata optimization. This research directly supports the decision framework for automated content opportunity scoring. By modeling historical movement, CTR under-capture, and impression volatility, we formulate an interpretable ranking system that flags high-upside pages requiring editorial intervention.

---

### 3. Data & Public Safety
- Dataset: Anonymized search performance dataset sourced from the FlyRank warehouse release (30,000+ pseudonymized URLs across 44 performance metrics).
- Date Windows & Coverage: Multi-week rolling aggregations examining historical impressions, clicks, average position, and session engagement.
- Exclusions: Sparse records with fewer than 10 total impressions and bot-flagged anomalies were filtered out to avoid synthetic noise.
- Public Safety Compliance: All client names, domain roots, specific keywords, and sensitive platform identifiers are completely masked and pseudonymized.

---

### 4. Methodology
- Label Definition: A binary target indicating whether a URL is in an actionable decline state or severe CTR under-capture relative to its SERP position band.
- Validation Split: A strict client-aware grouped split (80% train, 20% test) ensuring no client domain overlaps between training and evaluation, preventing cross-client data leakage.
- Feature Engineering: Features include rolling position velocity, impression delta, position-normalized CTR gap, and content decay index. Strict controls ensured neither target derivatives nor raw trend markers were leaked into feature inputs.
- Models Evaluated: Logistic Regression baseline compared against a tuned Random Forest and LightGBM classifier.

---

### 5. Results & Discussion
- Model Performance:
  * Heuristic Baseline: Precision @ Top 10% = 0.54, ROC-AUC = 0.68
  * Tuned Classifier: Precision @ Top 10% = 0.73, ROC-AUC = 0.78 (+0.10 AUC lift)
- Top Feature Drivers:
  1. Position-Normalized CTR Deficit (strongest predictor of missed opportunity)
  2. Rolling 30-Day Impression Acceleration
  3. SERP Volatility Coefficient
- Key Finding: Assets ranking in positions 4–10 exhibiting stable impressions but deteriorating CTR offer the highest immediate ROI from title and snippet refreshes.

---

### 6. Limitations & Honest Framing
- Framing: All findings represent observed directional associations and decision-support heuristics, not causal assertions about search engine ranking algorithms.
- Limitations: Data excludes off-page link distribution networks and real-time competitor algorithmic shifts, which can impact visibility independently of on-page signals.

---

### 7. Ranked Recommendations (Action Playbook)
1. Priority 1 (Quick Wins): Filter URLs in Positions 4–8 with negative CTR gap > 2.0% → Rewrite titles, meta descriptions, and structured snippet markup.
2. Priority 2 (Decay Mitigation): Pages with declining impressions over two consecutive rolling windows → Trigger structural content refresh and internal link consolidation.
3. Priority 3 (Maintain / Monitor): High-performing pages in Positions 1–3 with stable click capture → Preserve current metadata and schedule for passive monthly auditing.
